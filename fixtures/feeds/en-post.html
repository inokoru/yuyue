Notes on keeping a reading log

For a few years now I have kept a plain text file with one line for every book I finish: the date, the title, and a sentence about what stayed with me.

The habit costs almost nothing. The surprise is how useful the file becomes once it is long enough to search, because a single sentence written at the time is often more honest than anything I could reconstruct later.

If you try it, keep the format boring. A log you have to think about is a log you will stop writing.
