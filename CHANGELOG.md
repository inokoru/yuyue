## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-22

### zh
- 第一版：在 iPhone、iPad、Mac 和 Android 上读 EPUB、PDF、TXT、MOBI / AZW3，支持 Wi‑Fi 传书、监听文件夹和 OPDS / WebDAV 远程书库，书和笔记都留在你自己的设备上。
- 用本机离线声音、系统声音或你自己接的云端声音朗读，一句一句跟着高亮；锁屏和耳机都能控制。
- 分角色朗读与广播剧：认出每句台词是谁说的，给每个人物配不同的声音（支持本机克隆声音），整本提前做好，带语气、环境音和音效，能导出成有声书。
- 有声书：导入录音，和电子书一句一句对上，边听边看；没有电子书也能转成文字。
- 播客与书房电台：把外部播客（RSS、OPML、单集链接）或本地长音频排成可查词、可划原声切片的访谈书页；也能把在读的书和本周划线做成不剧透的书房电台，每句指回原文。
- 书伴：可用端侧模型、本机或局域网模型（Ollama、LM Studio 等）或接上你自己的大模型 API，默认只在读过的部分里回答，每句都指回原文。
- 记书与导出：划线、想法、生词本、今日回顾与书摘卡片；记录在读、读过、想读（纸书也能记）；导出到 Markdown、Obsidian、Notion、flomo、新枝、Anki、带划线的 EPUB 和阅读记录 CSV。
- 同步与快照：用 iCloud Drive 或 WebDAV 在设备之间同步进度、笔记和播客（冲突并排可自选，Google Drive 即将支持），支持整库数据快照导入导出。
- 应用内反馈：遇到问题或有建议可在书架、“我的”或菜单栏直接提交，邮箱、日志与私密附件先在本机加密再发出。

### en
- First release: read EPUB, PDF, TXT and MOBI / AZW3 on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, with Wi‑Fi transfer, watched folders and OPDS / WebDAV remote libraries; your books and notes stay on your own devices.
- Read aloud with local offline voices, system voices or a cloud voice you connect, highlighted sentence by sentence, with lock-screen and headphone controls.
- Character voices and audio drama: identify who speaks each line, assign a voice per character (including voices cloned on device), and render a whole book ahead with tone, room sound and effects, exportable as an audiobook.
- Audiobooks: import a recording and follow along sentence by sentence in the e-book; without one, transcribe the recording into text.
- Podcasts and studio radio: turn external podcasts (RSS, OPML, episode links) or local audio into an interview page where you can look up words and save audio clips; or generate a spoiler-free radio issue from what you are reading and highlighting this week, with every line linked back to the book.
- Reading companion: use an on-device model, a local or LAN server (Ollama, LM Studio and others) or your own cloud API key; answers stay within what you have read by default and cite the exact passage.
- Notes, book tracking and export: highlights, thoughts, word list, daily review and quote cards; track reading, read and to-read books (paper books included); export to Markdown, Obsidian, Notion, flomo, Xinzhi, Anki, highlighted EPUB and reading-log CSV.
- Sync and snapshots: sync progress, notes and podcasts across devices via iCloud Drive or WebDAV with side-by-side conflict resolution (Google Drive coming soon), plus full-library snapshot import and export.
- In-app feedback: send bug reports or suggestions right from the shelf, Me or the menu bar, with email, logs and private attachments encrypted on device before sending.
