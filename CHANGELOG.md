# 更新日志 · Changelog

Each version has a section, newest first. The app's changelog page and update notices come from here.

## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-18

### zh
- 第一版：在 iPhone、iPad、Mac 和 Android 上读 EPUB、PDF、TXT、MOBI，书和笔记都留在你自己的设备上。
- 划线、想法、生词本，笔记能导出到 Obsidian、Notion、flomo 和 Anki。
- 用系统声音或你自己接的云端声音朗读，一句一句跟着高亮。
- 分角色朗读：认出每句台词是谁说的，能给每个人物配不同的声音，也能用自己录的声音。
- 广播剧：整本提前做好，带语气、环境音和音效，能导出成有声书。
- 有声书：导入录音，和电子书一句一句对上，边听边看；没有电子书也能转成文字。
- 记书：在读、读过、想读，按章的进度，纸书也能记。
- 用 iCloud、WebDAV 或 Google Drive 在设备之间同步进度和笔记。

### en
- First release: read EPUB, PDF, TXT and MOBI on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, with your books and notes kept on your own devices.
- Highlights, thoughts and a word list; export notes to Obsidian, Notion, flomo and Anki.
- Read aloud with the system's voices or a cloud voice you connect, highlighted sentence by sentence.
- Voices by character: each line knows who says it, and each character can have a voice of its own, your recorded voice included.
- Audio drama: a whole book made ahead, with tone, room sound and effects, and exported as an audiobook.
- Audiobooks: import a recording and follow along sentence by sentence in the e-book; without one, turn the recording into text.
- Book tracking: reading, read and to read, progress by chapter, paper books too.
- Sync progress and notes between devices with iCloud, WebDAV or Google Drive.
